Dr. Qing Zhang is a Project Researcher at the <a href='https://ishiguro-lab.org/'>Ishiguro Lab</a> within the University of Tokyo's Interfaculty Initiative in Information Studies, and a Visiting Researcher at the Augmented Vision Lab, Institute of Science Tokyo. He previously served as a Project Assistant Professor at the <a href='https://lab.rekimoto.org/'>Rekimoto Lab</a>, The University of Tokyo. Drawing on a background that bridges fine art and technology—an M.F.A. from the Tokyo University of the Arts and a Ph.D. in Media Design from Keio University—his research sits at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction, wearable computing, and human-AI co-creation. He builds smart-eyewear systems that augment and modulate human visual perception ("programmable vision"), with applications spanning inclusive design for an aging society, eye-tracking and gaze interaction, skill acquisition, and 24/7 AI assistants that empower artists through Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs).
